## How to Get TC Location of Pauls Before TOT1?

![prelaunchproces](prelaunchprocess.png)

TC location data is critical information that needs to be collected after the vision casting or during the pre-launch visit.

The pre-launch visit serves as the best time to collect TC location, as TTI ministry leaders personally visit each Training Center to verify whether it meets TTI requirements.

The reporting team needs to stay in touch with the Ministry Team to communicate the importance of collecting TC location. The reporting team should also create the necessary forms to collect TC location and share them with the Ministry Team.

### Methods to Collect TC Location

- Creating an online form (Google Forms, Microsoft Forms)  
- Creating a shared Excel file  
- Using an app (GPScam, NoteCam) to capture the details  

---

## Why Do We Collect TC Locations Before TOT1?

The Training Center location of each Paul is collected before TOT1 so that the Data Entry Team can create the Training Centre Target Area (TCTA) during TOT1.

---

## Why Do We Need to Create TCTA for Every Paul?

TCTA helps Pauls focus their church planting efforts in villages surrounding their Training Center.

This enables Pauls to focus their ministry within nearby villages instead of traveling to distant locations.

Each Paul typically receives **35–40 villages** within their TCTA.

The TCTA map shows:

- Geographic boundaries of surrounding villages  
- Survey status of each village (Red, Yellow, Green, White)  

![TCTA](TCTA.png)


This is one of the approaches followed to accomplish **A Church In Every Village Everywhere**.

## 🧩 Phase 1 — Import TC and Create TCTA

To create TCTA for Pauls, Training Centers must first be available in iMetrics.

- Upload TC data to iMetrics (refer to *How to upload Training Centres* tutorial)  
- Once uploaded, proceed with creating/setting TCTA  

---

## 🧩 Phase 2 — Validate with Paul and MT

**Goal:** Ensure the TCTA geographic boundaries are accurate and verified by the Ministry Leader  

- **Establish Location:** Set the TCTA based strictly on the Training Center location, not the Paul’s residence (unless it is the Training Center)  
- **Paul Verification:** Present the generated TCTA map to the Paul for review  
- **Handle Feedback:** If the Paul reports missing villages or provides survey updates, do not make immediate changes  
- **Escalate:** Consult the Master Trainer (MT) to verify any feedback before proceeding  

> [!IMPORTANT]
> DET members should consult the MT because TOT1 is usually too early for Pauls to fully understand TCTA or the broader TTI framework. Updates should only be made when the data is verified  

---

## 🧩 Phase 3 — Update Based on Approved Feedback

**Goal:** Log verified data changes or maintain the current state based on MT authorization  

### Scenario A — MT Approves

1. Update the survey status or add the missing village in iMetrics  
2. Proceed to Phase 4  

### Scenario B — MT Disapproves / Delays

1. Make no changes in iMetrics  
2. Place the update on HOLD until the next monthly meeting or TOT2 for further verification  

---

## 🧩 Phase 4 — Print and Distribute TCTA

**Goal:** Provide the Paul with a physical or digital TCTA  

- **Standard Distribution:** Print the finalized TCTA map  
- **Security Protocol:** In high-persecution areas:
  - Do not distribute physical copies  
  - Provide soft copies instead  

Options:

- Full soft copy with all map pages  
- Single-page soft copy containing the village list only  

**Final Handover:**  
Ensure the Paul understands how to use the map for prayer, survey, and church planting
