> [!note] Important Guidelines for Field Visits
>  - Field Representative (FR) visits are planned and conducted independently as part of the regular reporting process, while maintaining coordination with ministry team.
>  - Zonal Leaders (ZLs) are welcome to accompany FRs during visits.  
>  - FRs must share their visit plans or schedules in advance with the Zonal Coordinator (ZC), Zonal Leader (ZL), and Point Person (PP) to ensure alignment and smooth coordination.  
>  - All teams are expected to extend full cooperation during FR visits to support effective verification and reporting.  
>  - Zonal Leaders and Point Persons must ensure that:
> 	 - All newly planted churches are communicated to the Reporting Team in a timely manner.  
> 	 - Every reported church plant is visited by a Field Representative.

## ==Collection==

As outlined in the flowchart above, the **Reporting Team** — including:

- Field Admins  
- Reporting Admins  

are responsible for collecting **Post-Graduate (PG) Church Plant reports** from:

- Point Persons  
- Team Leaders (TLs)  


### 📄 Submission Format

- Team Leaders may still use **Paul Report Forms** for submitting PG Church Plant data  
- This is **acceptable**, as the parameters remain the same  

### 🔄 Communication Protocol

- Maintain **continuous communication** with Team Leaders  
- Actively follow up for **regular updates and submissions**  

> [!Danger] ⚠️ Do not wait passively — proactive follow-up is required  


### 📊 Data Entry & Storage

- All reports must be recorded in the **designated Excel files** created for PG Church Plants  

#### 📁 File Locations

- **South Central (SC)**  
  `India (Reporting & Mapping) → South Central → SC_PMT_Reports`

- **North West (NW)**  
  `India (Reporting & Mapping) → North West → NW_PMT_Reports`

- **North East (NE)**  
  `India (Reporting & Mapping) → North East → NE_PMT_Reports`

---

### 👥 Roles & Responsibilities

#### 🧑‍💻 Data Entry
- Both **Field Admins** and **Reporting Admins** can:
  - Update the Excel file  
  - Enter data from **Paul Report Forms** received from Team Leaders  


---

### 📅 Follow-Up & Escalation Protocol

#### 🔁 Monthly Follow-Up
- Conduct follow-up **at the end of each month (28th – 31st)**  
- Ensure all Team Leaders submit their updates  

#### ⚠️ If No Response
1. Send follow-up reminders  
2. Continue engagement until response is received  

#### 🚨 Escalation Rule
- If there is still **no response after follow-up**:
  - Escalate to the **RLT Directors**  

---

### ⚠️ Critical Guidelines

- ❗ Do **NOT** collect reports at the end of the batch  
- ✅ Reports must be collected **monthly**  
- 🔁 Consistent follow-up is **mandatory**  

---

### 🧠 Key Principle

> 📌 *Consistent monthly collection ensures accuracy, accountability, and timely reporting*
