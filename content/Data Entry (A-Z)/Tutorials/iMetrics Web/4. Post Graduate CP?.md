## 1. ==Data Collection==

As outlined in the flowchart above, the **Reporting Team** — including:

- Field Admins  
- Reporting Admins  

are responsible for collecting **Post-Graduate (PG) Church Plant reports** from:

- Point Persons  
- Team Leaders (TLs)  

### 1. Submission Format

- Team Leaders may still use **Paul Report Forms** for submitting PG Church Plant data  
- This is **acceptable**, as the parameters remain the same  

### 2. Communication Protocol

- Maintain **continuous communication** with Team Leaders  
- Actively follow up for **regular updates and submissions**  

> [!Danger] ⚠️ Do not wait passively — proactive follow-up is required  


### 3. Data Entry & Storage

- All reports must be recorded in the **designated Excel files** created for PG Church Plants

#### File Locations

- **South Central (SC)**  
  `India (Reporting & Mapping) → South Central → SC_PMT_Reports`

- **North West (NW)**  
  `India (Reporting & Mapping) → North West → NW_PMT_Reports`

- **North East (NE)**  
  `India (Reporting & Mapping) → North East → NE_PMT_Reports`


### 4. Roles & Responsibilities

#### Data Entry
- Both **Field Admins** and **Reporting Admins** can:
  - Update the Excel file  
  - Enter data from **Paul Report Forms** received from Team Leaders  



### 5. Follow-Up & Escalation Protocol

#### Monthly Follow-Up
- Conduct follow-up **at the end of each month (28th – 31st)**  
- Ensure all Team Leaders submit their updates  

#### If No Response
1. Send follow-up reminders  
2. Continue engagement until response is received  

#### Escalation Rule
- If there is still **no response after follow-up**:
  - Escalate to the **RLT Directors**  


### 6. Critical Guidelines

- Do **NOT** collect reports at the end of the batch  
- Reports must be collected **monthly**  
- Consistent follow-up is **mandatory**  


### 🧠 Key Principle

> [!info] 📌 *Consistent monthly collection ensures accuracy, accountability, and timely reporting*

### Important Note ⚠️ 

For Post-Graduate Church Plant reporting It has been decided by the **Global Team** that the following records will not be tracked in iMetrics:

- New Believers  
- Baptisms  
- Orphans  
- Widows  

Only **Church Plant data (1G, 2G, 3G)** will be entered and tracked in iMetrics.


### Data Handling Requirement

Since iMetrics does not capture all parameters, it is **mandatory to record and retain the complete dataset** received from Team Leaders in the designated Excel file.

This includes:

- New Believers  
- Baptisms  
- Orphans  
- Widows  

> 📌 The Excel file serves as the **primary repository for all reporting data**, while iMetrics is used **only for Church Plant tracking**.

## Key Takeaways

- Reporting is a **continuous monthly process**, not a one-time activity

- **Timely data collection** is critical to prevent gaps, inaccuracies, and reporting delays  

- **Proactive follow-up** with Team Leaders is mandatory to ensure timely submissions  

- Data must be **verified for accuracy and completeness before entry into iMetrics**  

- **Reporting Admins are responsible for data validation, accuracy, and final entry into iMetrics**  

- **Field Admins support the process by collecting reports and updating the Excel records**  
- Delays or non-responses must be **escalated to RLT Directors if reporting timelines are not met**  

- The reporting process follows a **defined workflow: Collection → Verification → Data Entry**  

- **Delays at any stage impact the overall reporting system and data reliability**  

- Proper data handling ensures **accurate reporting, accountability, and effective decision-making**
