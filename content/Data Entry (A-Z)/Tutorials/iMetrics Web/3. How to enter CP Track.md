During TOTs (Training of Trainers), Data entry team collects the following data from the Pauls while keeping the Master Trainers ,Zonal Leaders, Zonal Coordinators in the loop. 
1. Paul Report Forms
2. Survey Data (Identifying the Gaps)
3. UPG data
**Paul Report Forms** basically says what has happened in the field (i.e. Churches Planted with location, New Believers, Baptisms, Orphans & Widows Served) for the past 3 months or in between the last TOT to the current TOT. Pauls enter the forms at the TOT (based on their journal) and some might bring it filled. 

Data entry team members are required to collect the various data mentioned above from the Pauls and enter the data to iMetrics. 


> This article explains how to enter Paul Report Forms (i.e. CP Record) received from Pauls during TOTs to iMetrics


Process:
Collection: As mentioned above it is very important to collect forms filled by the Pauls and checked by the Master Trainers, many times a Master Trainer might not be aware of what a Paul is reporting so a Master Trainer should know what is being reported for his Paul for places where we do not have Master Trainers Zonal Leaders can review. This process is important as we as reporting team want the ministry leaders to be aware of what is being entered in our systems (just to be in good terms). There is another important point to be noted here that it is not neccessary for a Master Trainer or Zonal Leader to have visited a Church Plant by a Paul (i.e CP under a Paul) to be reported in other words if MT, ZL have not visited a Church Plant but if a Paul has visited/verified a church planted by his Timothy then a Paul can fill in the Paul report form and submit.

Verification: After collecting Paul Report Form Data entry team needs to check all forms, The following are the metrics to check before uploading to iMetrics
1. Check if Timothy names mentioned in the Pauls report form matches with what is already listed in iMetrics
2. Check the name of village (village should be as per iShare). If after checking that a village is missing then follow process of "Adding a Missing village"
