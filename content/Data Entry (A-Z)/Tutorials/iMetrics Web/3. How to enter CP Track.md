During TOTs (Training of Trainers), Data entry team collects the following data from the Pauls while keeping the Master Trainers ,Zonal Leaders, Zonal Coordinators in the loop. 
1. Paul Report Forms
2. Survey Data (Identifying the Gaps)
3. UPG data
**Paul Report Forms** basically says what has happened in the field (i.e. Churches Planted with location, New Believers, Baptisms, Orphans & Widows Served) for the past 3 months or in between the last TOT to the current TOT. Pauls enter the forms at the TOT (based on their journal) and some might bring it filled. 

Data entry team members are required to collect the various data mentioned above from the Pauls and enter the data to iMetrics. 


> This article explains how to enter Paul Report Forms (i.e. CP Record) received from Pauls during TOTs to iMetrics


Process:
Collection: As mentioned above it is very important to collect forms filled by the Pauls and checked by the Master Trainers, many times a Master Trainer might not be aware of what a Paul is reporting so a Master Trainer should know what is being reported for his Paul for places where we do not have Master Trainers Zonal Leaders can review. This process is important as we as reporting team want the ministry leaders to be aware of what is being entered in our systems (just to be in good terms). There is another important point to be noted here that it is not neccessary for a Master Trainer or Zonal Leader to have visited a Church Plant by a Paul (i.e CP under a Paul) to be reported in other words if MT, ZL have not visited a Church Plant but if a Paul has visited/verified a church planted by his Timothy then a Paul can fill in the Paul report form and submit.

Verification: After collecting Paul Report Form Data entry team needs to check all forms, The following are the metrics to check before uploading to iMetrics
1. Check if Timothy names mentioned in the Pauls report form matches with what is already listed in iMetrics
2. The following needs to checked in terms of location of Church Plant,
	1. Check the name of village (village should be as per iShare). Sometimes a Paul might write the village name but it won't be the official name, it would be the name that the locals use, in such cases it is difficult to find it in the iShare records.
	2. Sometimes a paul may write the name of the village but he/she might not know under which block (level 3) that village falls under and may write the block name as per his understanding and if a Data entry team member encounters this he may rule it out as Missing village which is incorrect. 
	3. It is very likely that the village (which the paul has mentioned) falls under some other block or district so due diligence needs to be done by the data entry team members before ruling it as a "missing village" and proceeding to [[2. Add a missing village| Adding Missing Villages]].
3. Check for the number of new believers, baptisms. Sometimes a Paul might report baptisms higher than new believers
4. Check if a paul has reported 2nd Generation Church Plant without mentioning Titus'
5. Check the dates, sometimes a Paul might use dd-mm--yyyy format while some other paul might use mm-dd--yyyy or dd--yyyy--mm format. 
whenever such issues or problems occur during verification then always go back to the Paul, while keeping the Master Trainers ,Zonal Leaders, Zonal Coordinators in the loop and get the correct information and then proceed to the next step.

A rule of thumb: When in doubt or if something doesnt make sense then clarify don't proceed.

Entering Data: Entering of the data should be always done after verification, else there is a high probability of entering wrong information. The following section explains how to enter Church Plant data in iMetrics
1. Step 1: 
	1. Go to the Paul's profile for whom you want to add Church Plant/s then go to "Churches" section. Click on "Add Church" button. 
	2. Alternatively you can also click on the "+" icon next to a Timothy's name in "Timothies and Churches" tab and it takes you to the same page as mentioned in point 1 with the exception being that a Timothy name is already entered in the form which is not the case if point 1 is followed. 
	3. See images below
2. Step 2: You are greeted with "New Church" form. 
	1. If adding a 1st Generation Church (i.e. Church Planted by Timothy) then select "Choose an existing Church Planter" from the dropdown where you can choose a Timothy.
	2. if adding a 2nd Generation Church (i.e. Church Planted by Titus) then select "Choose a Church Planter Mentor" and choose the name of the Timothy from the dropdown and write the name of the Titus in the "Enter New Church Planter Name"
	3. See images below
3. Step 3: Description is Optional to write but if you are entering Church Plant data for Post-Graduate batch (applicable for India only) then it is recommended to write "Post-Graduate Church Plant" in the description it just helps whenever someone is going through the church records of a Paul
4. Step 4:Write the date in the specified format. This should be not a problem if the verification stage was followed. 
5. Step 5: Estimated Size refers to the size or the number of people attending the church which is being entered. This field won't accept values less than 3 (Read [[Reporting Terms]] for a better understanding of the definition of a Church.
6. Step 6: Location field is where we enter the village, block, district of the Church Plant. This is a very crucial field as it matters a lot to track our ACHIEVE movement. So therefore its crucial that we do the neccessary due diligence mentioned in above in the verification stage. Follow the process below to add a location for a Church Plant.
	1. Click on "Select a Location"
	2. A new popup "Choose a Church Location" opens up. To add a village where the church was planted 
		1. You can search in the "Search" bar for the village that you are looking for or
		2. Go through the levels (Level 1- State, Level 2- District, Level 3- Block, Level 4- Village
		3. Select the location directly by clicking on the map
7. Click on "Save Location" and the "Choose a Church Location" popup closes.
8. To save the church record click on "Create Church" button
9. The Church record created will be displayed on the Pauls Profile under the "Churches" Tab
