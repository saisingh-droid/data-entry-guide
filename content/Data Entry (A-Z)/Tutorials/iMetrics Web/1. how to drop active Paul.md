## What Does "Dropping a Paul" Mean?

A **Paul is considered "dropped"** when he/she discontinues the 2-year curriculum program and is no longer actively participating.

This may occur for several reasons, including:
- Loss of interest  
- Feeling overwhelmed or intimidated (due to processes or requirements)  
- Death  

Once the ministry confirms that a Paul will not continue, the **Reporting Team** / **Data Entry Team** is responsible for updating the status in iMetrics accordingly.

---

## Process to Drop an Active Paul

Follow the steps below to update the status of an active Paul:

1. Navigate to the **Training Center Group** page  
2. Select the **Paul’s name** from the list  
3. Click the **"Edit"** button (top-right corner)  
4. In the **"Training Center Status"** dropdown, select **"Dropped"**  
5. Provide the required details when prompted:
   - Select a **Reason for Dropping** from the dropdown  
   - Enter details in the **"Explanation for Dropping"** field  
   - Specify the **"Date Dropped"**  

6. Click **"Update Training Centre"** to save the changes
