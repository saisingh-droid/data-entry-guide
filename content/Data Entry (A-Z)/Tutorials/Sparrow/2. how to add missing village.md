## How to Perform Additional Checks / Validation Before Marking Village(s) as **Missing**

  
> [!tip]
> Perform these steps **only after fully understanding the required “Additional Checks.”**
> This section explains _how_ to execute those checks.

### Method 1: Verify with the Paul

When the Data Entry Team (DET) or Mapping Specialist is unable to locate a village, the **first step** is to consult the Paul. Ensure the MT and ZL are kept in the loop.

#### Steps to Follow

1. **Contact the Paul directly** and confirm:
   - Whether the village name provided is the **official name**
   - If the village has any **alternate/local names (aliases)**

2. **Validate location details:**
   - Confirm the **Block (Level 3)**
   - Confirm the **District (Level 2)**
   - Check if any **administrative details were entered incorrectly**

3. **Check for jurisdiction changes:**
   - A new **taluk/block** may have been formed
   - The village may now fall under a **different Block or District**

4. **Re-attempt search in iMetrics:**
   - Use the corrected details to locate and **select the village**
   - If found → ✅ Proceed with normal workflow  
   - If not found → ❌ Move to the next validation method
