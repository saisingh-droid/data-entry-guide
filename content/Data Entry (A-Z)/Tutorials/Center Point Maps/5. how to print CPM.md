> [!IMPORTANT]
> This document explains the different ways to print **TCTA (CPM)** .
> Printing CPM may vary depending on the context or security requirements of a region. Therefore, it is important to understand all available printing methods.



> [!note] Prerequisites
> Ensure the following steps are completed before printing TCTA:
> - TCTA must be correctly set in the **Paul’s Profile Page** 
> 	- → See: [[2. how to set CPM]]
> - Survey data must be entered 
> 	- → See: [[4. how to enter surveys]]
> - Church records must be entered  
> 	- → See: [[3. how to enter CP Track]]

---

## 1. Printing TCTA (Standard Method)


> [!danger] Use this method when printing can be done normally without security concerns

<br>

- Click on the **TCTA** section in the Paul’s profile

- Click on the **"Generate PDF"** button


---

## 2. Printing TCTA (Secure Method)


> [!danger] Use this method in regions where additional caution is required

<br>

- Click on the **TCTA** section in the Paul’s profile
  

- Ensure **☑ 🔒 Secure** is enabled  ![[secureuiMetrics.png|image showing secure unchecked]]![[secureciMetrics.png|image showing secure checked]] 
  

- Click on the **"Generate PDF"** button 
  

|![[TCTAiMetrics.png]]|![[TCTA2iMetrics.png]]|
|-|-



---

## 3. Printing TCTA (Most Secure Method)


> [!danger] Use this method only in high-risk regions where strict security measures are required.  
> This is recommended in areas with a high risk of persecution.


<br>

- Follow the same process as [[#2. Printing TCTA (Secure Method)]]

- After the TCTA PDF is generated:
	- Do **not** share/print **Page 1 (Map View)**
	- Share/print only **Page 2 onwards (Village List)**


![[mostsec.gif]]
‍

---


> [!NOTE]
> These are the three methods available for printing **CPM (TCTA)** .
> Every member of the **DET team** should be familiar with these approaches.
>
> The final decision on which method to use (Standard, Secure, or Most Secure) is made by the **RLT leaders**. However, **Reporting Managers** may provide recommendations based on their understanding of the country or region.
