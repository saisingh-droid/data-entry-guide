## Village Allocation 
The number of villages assigned to a CPM depends on several factors. It is essential to consult the **RLT team** to determine the appropriate number of villages for each CPM. * 
**Performance Benchmark:** In some countries, a range of **35–40 villages** has proven to be effective. 

### Feasibility Considerations 
Feasibility must also be considered, particularly the **distance from the TC**. 

> [!WARNING]
> If a selected village is too far, it may discourage the Paul or create an unnecessary burden. 


---

## 🤝 Final Decision

No village allocation should be finalized without:

- Validation from the **RLT team**  
- Agreement from the **Paul**

> [!tip]
> Involving both ensures the plan is practical, realistic, and sustainable.
