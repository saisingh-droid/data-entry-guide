> [!INFO]
> To streamline survey efforts for COTW, two new tools have been introduced:
> - **Survey Administration Tool**
> - **Offline Survey Mobile Application**
> 
> These tools work in harmony with each other to make survey data more readily accessible within **iShare**.

<br>

# Understanding the Tools and Their Purpose

## 1. Survey Administration Tool (Falcon Web)

**Survey Administration Tool** (also known as **Falcon Web**) is a web-based platform used by users with administrative access, such as:
- 👤 Field Representative Managers  
- 👥 Team Leaders  
- 📊 Reporting Managers  

## ⚙️ Key Functions

- 🏘️ Create clusters of villages to be surveyed  
- 📈 Monitor survey progress  
- ✏️ Modify cluster details  
- 🚀 Publish clusters for field use  



## 2. Offline Survey Mobile Application (Falcon Mobile)

**Offline Survey Mobile Application** (also known as **Falcon Mobile**) is a mobile app primarily used by Field Representatives to conduct surveys in the field.

## ⚙️ Key Functions

- ⬇️ Download assigned clusters *(assigned through Falcon Web)*  
- 🧭 Conduct surveys offline  
- ☁️ Upload survey data *(completed, partially completed, or skipped)* to the cloud *(Falcon Web)*  
- 🗑️ Remove assigned clusters from the device *(important for data security)*  

---



> [!NOTE]
> **Falcon Web (Survey Administration Tool)** and **Falcon Mobile (Offline Survey Mobile Application)** are not separate tools. They are two components of the same system.  
> Falcon Web is used to manage and administer survey clusters, while Falcon Mobile is used to conduct field surveys.
