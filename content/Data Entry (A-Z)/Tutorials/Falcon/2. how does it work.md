## How the Survey Tools Work Together

![[Falcon.SurveyFlow.png]]

The diagram above illustrates how the Survey Administration Tool and the Offline Survey Mobile Application work together. The overall process is as follows:

### 1. Survey Planning and Assignment

A Survey Administrator uses the **Survey Administration Tool (Falcon Web)** to:
- Identify villages that need to be surveyed  
- Group these villages into **Survey Batches**  
- Assign each Survey Batch to a field surveyor  

> A **Survey Batch** is a group of villages assigned to a single surveyor for data collection.

Each Survey Batch contains a set of villages for a surveyor to complete.

---

### 2. Field Data Collection

Surveyors use the **Offline Survey Mobile Application (Falcon Mobile)** on their mobile devices to conduct surveys.

- Only the Survey Batches assigned to them are visible in the app  
- Surveys can be conducted offline  
- Once all villages in a batch are surveyed, the surveyor:
  - Marks the batch as complete  
  - Uploads the data for review  

---

### 3. Review and Publishing

The Survey Administrator then:
- Reviews the submitted Survey Batches  
- Makes any necessary adjustments  
- Publishes the finalized survey results to **iShare**  

---

### 4. Data Usage in iShare

COTW members access the published survey data in **iShare** to:
- Support planning and decision-making  
- Collaborate with other teams and organizations  
- Advance ministry efforts  

---

> [!SUMMARY]
> The system follows a simple flow:  
> **Plan → Assign → Survey → Review → Publish → Use in iShare**
