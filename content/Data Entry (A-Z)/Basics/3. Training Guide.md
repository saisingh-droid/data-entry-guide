

## Purpose of This Document

> [!NOTE]
> This document serves as a comprehensive training manual for selected members of the Data Entry Team (DET).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The training must be conducted by the Reporting Manager to ensure that it is properly contextualized according to the specific needs and circumstances of his or her country.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Data entry is a mission-critical function during TOTs and directly supports the broader vision and reporting accuracy of ACHIEVE. Since reporting outcomes, mapping accuracy, and data integrity depend heavily on the DET, members must be fully trained and operationally ready well in advance.

---

## 1. Training Objectives

By the end of the training, each DET member should be able to:

1. Accurately enter data related to Church Plant (track) and surveys  
2. Create, modify, and delete target areas correctly  
3. Understand every "ACHIEVE Tech Tool" and its function  
4. Identify and troubleshoot common data entry errors  
5. Follow standardized workflows/processes during TOTs  
6. Maintain data integrity, accuracy, and confidentiality  
7. Coordinate effectively with the Reporting Manager, Training Coordinator, and Ministry team  

---

## 2. Training Timeline & Structure

### Suggested Structure

#### Phase 1: Conceptual Foundation

1. Understanding TOT reporting objectives  
2. Overview of the ACHIEVE reporting framework  
3. Roles and responsibilities of the DET  

#### Phase 2: Tool Familiarization

1. Hands-on training with reporting platforms (e.g., mapping tools)  
2. Understanding dashboards, validation checks, and backend logic  
3. Understanding forms (Paul Report, Survey, UPG)  
4. Understanding how iMetrics, Falcon, and Sparrow connect together  

#### Phase 3: Live Practice

1. Mock data entry exercises (Paul reports, surveys, missing villages, UPG)  
2. Error correction drills (identifying common errors)  
3. Supervised real data entry (if possible)  
4. Practice with past datasets  

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Training should ideally begin at least three months prior to the scheduled TOT.

## 3. Core Training Components

### 3.1 Tools & Systems

#### iMetrics

- [ ] Understanding the layout (Country → TC Group → Pauls → Timothy)  
- [ ] How to enter UPG, survey, and Church Plant data  
- [ ] Setting TC location → setting TCTA village → target area management  
- [ ] Printing TCTA (secure vs. unsecure)  
- [ ] Adding and dropping (Pauls, Timothys)  
- [ ] Handling name corrections (spellings)  
- [ ] Church Plant drop, corrections/updates (location, New Believers)  
- [ ] Reading/interpreting statistics  
- [ ] Maintaining reports (Excel, OneDrive)  
- [ ] Reading/interpreting dashboards/charts  

#### Falcon

- [ ] General overview of what Falcon is, why Falcon is used, and how it works  
- [ ] How to assign clusters, manage offline surveys, upload data, and delete  

#### Sparrow

- [ ] General overview of what Sparrow is, why Sparrow is used, and how it works  
- [ ] Identifying missing villages during data entry  
- [ ] Process of verifying that a village is truly missing in the system  
- [ ] Submission process for adding a village to Sparrow  
- [ ] Approval process after submission  
- [ ] Surveying a village after it is added to the map  
- [ ] Understanding latitude and longitude  
- [ ] How to obtain latitude/longitude data  
- [ ] Troubleshooting (when things do not go as expected)  

#### iShare

- [ ] General overview of what iShare is, why iShare is used, and how it works  
- [ ] Survey entry (backup process when iMetrics does not work)  
- [ ] Map printouts

## 4. Assessment

Before deployment, each DET member should:

- Practice TOT data entry in the DEMO site  
- Members who are not confident in any part of data entry need to be retrained  

---

## 5. Roles, Accountability & Communication

- Clearly define team roles (Reporting Manager → Mapping Specialist → Data Entry Lead → Data Entry Member, etc.)  
- Establish reporting hierarchy and escalation protocols  
- Maintain structured communication channels  
  - Daily check-ins during TOT  
  - End-of-day assessment (what was accomplished and what remains)  
- Document issues and resolutions systematically  

---

## 6. Post-TOT Evaluation

- FR/Reporting Managers conduct post-TOT review meetings to improve future processes  

---

## 7. Continuous Improvement

DET member training should not be a one-time event. After each TOT:

- Conduct performance reviews  
- Identify recurring errors  
- Update training materials accordingly  
- Incorporate lessons learned into the next training cycle  

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The effectiveness of a TOT reporting process depends significantly on the preparedness of the Data Entry Team. A well-trained DET ensures data accuracy, operational efficiency, and reliable measurement of outcomes—ultimately strengthening the integrity of ACHIEVE reporting.
